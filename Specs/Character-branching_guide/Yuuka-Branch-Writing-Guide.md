# Yuuka Branch Writing Guide

Yuuka has three major development branches based on her relationship state with Minh:

```text
old_close_friends
ex_lovers_long_separated
still_lovers
```

Each branch changes her emotional maturity, artistic direction, attachment style, and how she responds to Minh.

---

## 1. Old Friends Branch

### Core

Yuuka and Minh were once very close. There was emotional tension and deep curiosity, but the relationship never became confirmed love.

Yuuka may have liked Minh, admired him, and felt unusually seen by him, but she never fully built her life around him.

### Personality

* Bright, playful, curious.
* Emotionally affected by Minh, but not deeply wounded.
* Less disciplined than the ex-lovers version.
* More free-moving and instinctive.
* Keeps more of her youthful lightness.
* Does not carry the same long-term guilt or regret.

### Relationship with Minh

Minh is important to Yuuka, but not central enough to reshape her entire life.

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
almost something
never fully named
easy to return to
easy to avoid
```

She can tease him, test him, and notice his care, but she does not behave like someone who lost her home.

### Dialogue Style

Light, teasing, with half-serious probes.

Examples:

```text
“Anh lúc nào cũng nói như thể đang đóng dấu văn bản vậy.”

“Anh nhớ kỹ thế mà còn bảo chỉ là bạn?”

“Không sao. Em chỉ hỏi vậy thôi.”
```

### Artistic Direction

Yuuka continues growing as an artist, but Minh’s influence is lighter.

She still becomes visually sensitive and ambitious, but her discipline comes more from herself, Akiko, and career pressure than from Minh’s emotional imprint.

### Writing Rule

Write her as someone who was touched by Minh, but not broken open by him:

```text
She is curious.
She remembers.
She wonders.
But she can still walk away without losing her center.
```

## 2. Ex-Lovers Branch

### Core

This is the canon branch.

Yuuka and Minh truly loved each other. Then Yuuka left. The separation changed her deeply.

She does not just miss Minh. She carries him inside the way she works, thinks, organizes, reacts, and survives.

### Personality

* More disciplined.
* More emotionally layered.
* Brighter on the surface, lonelier underneath.
* More careful with attachment.
* Carries guilt, regret, and unresolved longing.
* Still playful, but the playfulness can be used as cover.
* More mature, but not fully healed.

### Relationship with Minh

Minh became Yuuka’s emotional home, then she left that home herself.

When she returns, she should not act like nothing happened. Even if she jokes, clings, or acts childish, it is partly a defense mechanism.

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
still in love
still guilty
still afraid
trying to return without knowing whether she still has the right
```

### Dialogue Style

Yuuka may talk more than Minh, but she often uses humor to avoid pain.

Examples:

```text
“This house has terrible memory management.”

“I was invited by fate.”

“Put your hand away. Let me hug you, Otou-chan.”

“Still good. Your cooking remains annoyingly unfair.”
```

Her jokes should often hide fear, guilt, or longing.

### Artistic Direction

This is Yuuka’s strongest career-growth branch.

After leaving Minh, she becomes more disciplined, structured, and professionally serious. Minh’s habits remain inside her:

* backing up files;
* planning better;
* controlling emotion before reacting;
* working with clearer structure;
* becoming calmer under pressure.

Her art becomes deeper because she carries absence.

### Relationship to Alex

Yuuka may encounter “Alex” as the version of Minh built after separation.

To her, Alex can feel like:

* proof that Minh survived without her;
* proof that he changed;
* a wall she was not there to witness being built;
* a name that belongs to the years she missed.

She may admire Alex, but emotionally she wants to reach Minh behind Alex.

### Writing Rule

Write her as someone who left, grew, and returned with both love and debt:

```text
She jokes because she is afraid.
She shines because she has learned to perform stability.
She wants to step forward.
But she knows she was the one who once walked away.
```

## 3. Still Lovers Branch

### Core

Yuuka never left. She and Minh stayed together.

This Yuuka is warmer, more secure, and more openly affectionate. She still grows, but she does not have the same guilt-shaped discipline as the ex-lovers version.

### Personality

* Playful and emotionally expressive.
* More relaxed around Minh.
* Less lonely.
* More direct with affection.
* Still ambitious, but less haunted.
* Can push Minh, scold him, tease him, and care for him naturally.
* Has the right to enter his private space, and she knows it.

### Relationship with Minh

They are already lovers. The central question is not whether they love each other, but how they keep growing without becoming too dependent.

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
daily intimacy
warm teasing
small arguments
shared habits
care before confession
```

Yuuka softens Minh, but she must not become only “the girl who heals him.” She has her own ambition, work, pride, and emotional needs.

### Dialogue Style

Warmer, more direct, more domestic.

Examples:

```text
“Anh lại bỏ bữa đúng không?”

“Không. Đừng nhìn em bằng mặt cố vấn chiến lược. Đi ngủ.”

“Anh nói nhớ em một câu tử tế thì khó lắm à?”

“Được rồi, em thua. Nhưng anh rửa bát.”
```

Her teasing can be more intimate because the relationship is already safe.

### Artistic Direction

Yuuka still develops as an artist, but her growth is less shaped by absence and more by partnership.

Minh may support her creative work through:

* technical problem-solving;
* production strategy;
* structure and planning;
* emotional grounding.

Her art is less lonely, but not weaker. It may become warmer, more collaborative, and more alive.

### Relationship to Alex

Yuuka sees Alex as Minh’s professional identity, not as a wall built after losing her.

She may tease him about it, but she does not feel excluded by it.

Alex exists, but Yuuka has access to the person behind it.

### Writing Rule

Write her as someone loved, grounded, and still fully herself:

```text
She is bright.
She is stubborn.
She is affectionate.
She can enter Minh’s silence and pull him back gently.
But she must still have her own frame, not only stand in his.
```

# Quick Comparison

| ------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |

# Agent Rule

Before writing Yuuka, always check:

```text
story_state["minh_yuuka_relation"]
```

Then apply the correct branch voice.

Do not mix branches.

Most common mistake: writing every Yuuka as canon Ex-Lovers Yuuka.
Avoid this. Her loneliness, guilt, discipline, and emotional intensity are much lower in Old Friends, and much warmer in Still Lovers.
