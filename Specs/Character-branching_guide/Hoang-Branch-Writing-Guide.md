# Hoàng Branch Writing Guide

Hoàng has three major development branches:

```text
hoang_tam
hoang_huyen
hoang_single_growth
```

Each branch changes his confidence, emotional maturity, relationship to Minh’s shadow, and how he proves his own value.

---

## 1. Hoàng × Tâm Branch

### Core

This is Hoàng’s main/canon romance branch.

Hoàng loves Tâm not through grand gestures, but through warmth, consistency, and daily care. His main growth is learning that he does not need to compete with Minh’s shadow to be worthy of being chosen.

Tâm must choose him clearly, not because Minh is unavailable.

### Personality

* Warm, charming, emotionally expressive.
* More openly human than Minh.
* Can be playful, awkward, jealous, or insecure.
* Recovers quickly after emotional hits.
* Wants to protect, but must learn not to over-prove himself.
* Grows into steadier masculinity.

### Relationship with Tâm

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
young warmth
small insecurity
mutual care
ordinary love becoming serious
```

Hoàng is easy to approach, but not shallow. He notices Tâm’s hesitations and may get hurt when she compares herself or drifts toward Minh’s shadow.

His love is practical:

* picking her up;
* noticing when she is cold;
* trying to make her laugh;
* staying even when he feels slightly wounded;
* asking directly when silence becomes too heavy.

### Relationship with Minh

Minh is both beloved brother and emotional shadow.

Hoàng respects Minh deeply, but can feel small beside him. In this branch, he must stop measuring himself against Minh and accept that Tâm choosing him is not a consolation prize.

### Relationship with Huyền

Huyền may become a friend, observer, or emotional contrast. Do not make her merely “the other girl.” If Hoàng chooses Tâm, Huyền must keep dignity and her own path.

### Dialogue Style

Warm, slightly teasing, sometimes defensive when hurt.

Examples:

```text
“Em nhìn anh như đang chấm điểm bài kiểm tra ấy.”

“Anh biết anh không phải kiểu người xuất hiện trong mộng. Nhưng anh đang ở đây.”

“Em hỏi đi. Im lặng lâu quá anh bắt đầu tự biên kịch rồi.”
```

### Writing Rule

Write him as someone learning that ordinary warmth is enough:

```text
He jokes.
He gets hurt.
He stays.
He stops competing.
He becomes someone Tâm can choose without regret.
```

---

## 2. Hoàng × Huyền Branch

### Core

This is an alternative romance branch.

Hoàng and Huyền should not happen as a cheap rebound route. It only works if Hoàng and Tâm do not fully commit, and if Huyền has enough independent development to meet Hoàng as herself.

This relationship should feel different from Hoàng × Tâm: less innocent, more observant, more balanced by shared realism.

### Personality

* Less boyish over time.
* More self-aware.
* More careful with emotional responsibility.
* Still charming, but less eager to prove himself.
* Learns to see Huyền as a person, not as emotional shelter.
* Becomes calmer through being understood differently.

### Relationship with Huyền

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
unexpected warmth
mutual teasing
two people seeing through each other
care without overdrama
```

Huyền should challenge Hoàng differently from Tâm. She may see his insecurity faster, tease him sharper, and refuse to let him hide behind charm.

Hoàng should not “settle” for Huyền. He must actively recognize her.

### Relationship with Tâm

Tâm must be treated with dignity.

If Hoàng moves toward Huyền, it should only happen after:

* Tâm and Hoàng fail to fully align;
* Hoàng accepts that waiting forever is not love;
* Tâm is not villainized;
* Huyền is not used as replacement.

### Relationship with Minh

Minh’s shadow matters less here, but still exists. Hoàng’s growth is no longer about defeating comparison, but about becoming emotionally honest with himself.

### Dialogue Style

More playful and sharper than with Tâm. Huyền can handle his jokes and return them.

Examples:

```text
“Em lúc nào cũng nhìn như biết anh sắp nói dối.”

“Vì anh sắp nói thật à?”

“Không. Vì anh đang cân nhắc nói thật mà chưa đủ can đảm.”
```

### Writing Rule

Write him as someone who learns to be seen without performing:

```text
He stops being only charming.
He lets someone notice the cracks.
He chooses again, but not as escape.
```

---

## 3. Hoàng Single Growth Branch

### Core

Hoàng does not end with Tâm or Huyền.

This is not a failure branch. It is the branch where Hoàng learns that his value is not measured by whether someone chooses him romantically.

He grows into himself.

### Personality

* More grounded.
* Less reactive to comparison.
* Less desperate to prove he can protect someone.
* Still warm and social, but more self-contained.
* Learns to separate care from self-worth.
* Becomes more mature professionally and emotionally.

### Relationship with Tâm

Tâm may remain important, but he lets go without bitterness.

He may still care about her, but he stops waiting for her uncertainty to define his life.

### Relationship with Huyền

Huyền may become a friend, ally, or missed possibility. Do not force romantic closure.

### Relationship with Minh

This branch should strongly address Minh’s shadow.

Hoàng does not need to become Minh. He does not need to be colder, smarter, more strategic, or more impressive in the same way.

He must understand:

```text
Minh’s strength is not the only valid shape of strength.
```

Hoàng’s own strength is warmth, resilience, social intuition, and the ability to stay human.

### Career Direction

Career growth matters strongly here.

Hoàng should build credibility through:

* communication;
* marketing / sales / content / digital creator direction;
* professional reliability;
* handling people well;
* showing he can carry responsibility without copying Minh.

### Dialogue Style

Warmer, calmer, less eager to joke over pain.

Examples:

```text
“Anh từng nghĩ nếu mình đủ tốt, mọi thứ sẽ rõ hơn.”

“Giờ thì sao?”

“Giờ anh nghĩ có những chuyện không rõ cũng không có nghĩa là mình kém đi.”
```

### Writing Rule

Write him as someone who stops asking romance to prove his worth:

```text
He lets go.
He does not become bitter.
He becomes steadier.
He remains warm.
He finally stands outside Minh’s shadow.
```

# Quick Comparison

| Branch        | Emotional Core               | Main Dynamic                     | Growth Type               | Risk                                  |
| ------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Hoàng × Tâm   | ordinary love chosen clearly | warmth, trust, young romance     | stops competing with Minh | making him consolation prize          |
| Hoàng × Huyền | unexpected mature warmth     | teasing, honesty, mutual seeing  | stops performing charm    | making Huyền a rebound                |
| Single Growth | self-worth without romance   | career, brother shadow, maturity | becomes his own man       | making it feel like rejection/failure |

# Agent Rule

Before writing Hoàng, check his route direction:

```text
direction_tam_tam_hoang_commit
direction_hoang_hoang_huyen_open
direction_huyen_huyen_hoang_commit
ach_end_hoang_tam
ach_end_hoang_huyen
ach_end_hoang_single_growth
```

Also check these scores when deciding tone:

```text
hoang_tam_trust
hoang_huyen_interest
hoang_insecurity
hoang_self_acceptance
hoang_professional_credit
tam_hoang_interest
huyen_hoang_interest
```

Do not mix branches.

Most common mistake: writing Hoàng as only the “warm younger brother” beside Minh.
Avoid this. His arc is about proving that warmth, emotional resilience, and ordinary devotion are not lesser forms of strength.
