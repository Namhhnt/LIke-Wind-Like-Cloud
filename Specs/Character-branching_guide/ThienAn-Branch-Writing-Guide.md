# Thiên An Branch Writing Guide

Thiên An has four major development branches:

```text
thienan_quang
thienan_minh
thienan_single
thienan_arranged
```

Each branch changes her relationship to responsibility, family pressure, emotional vulnerability, and her ability to receive help.

---

## 1. Thiên An × Quang Branch

### Core

This is Thiên An’s healthiest romance branch.

Quang is not someone who saves her by force. He is someone who stays close enough for her to finally learn that receiving help is not weakness.

Thiên An’s arc here is about allowing trust without turning it into debt.

### Personality

* Competent, controlled, and responsible.
* Still proud, but less rigid over time.
* Learns to rely on someone without losing authority.
* Allows small vulnerability in private.
* Keeps her elegance and discipline, but becomes warmer.

### Relationship with Quang

Quang sees the tired person behind Thiên An’s capable surface.

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
quiet loyalty
professional trust
emotional patience
help offered without pressure
love built through staying
```

Quang should not push too hard. Thiên An should not surrender too easily. The relationship grows because he proves, repeatedly, that he can help without taking ownership of her life.

### Dialogue Style

Restrained, mature, with subtle softness.

Examples:

```text
“Anh không cần làm đến mức đó.”

“Không cần, hay là em chưa quen để người khác làm?”

“Quang.”

“Anh nghe.”
```

```text
“Nếu tôi nhận giúp đỡ lần này, anh định đòi gì?”

“Đòi em ngủ đủ một đêm.”
```

### Writing Rule

Write her as someone learning that strength does not require isolation:

```text
She leads.
She resists.
She receives.
She remains herself.
```

## 2. Thiên An × Minh Branch

### Core

This is a rare, emotionally heavy alternative branch.

It only works if Minh and Yuuka are in the `old_close_friends` branch and Minh is not romance-locked to Yuuka.

Thiên An and Minh are both people shaped by responsibility, control, and hidden exhaustion. Their relationship is not bright or easy. It is built on recognition.

### Personality

* More emotionally exposed than usual, but only in controlled ways.
* Sharp, composed, and difficult to approach.
* Drawn to Minh because he understands pressure without needing explanation.
* Risks mistaking shared burden for intimacy.
* Must choose from clarity, not old residue.

### Relationship with Minh

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
two controlled people
recognizing each other’s weight
speaking around old wounds
care hidden under competence
```

Minh should not become a rescue fantasy. Thiên An should not become “the woman who understands him better than Yuuka.” This branch only works if it respects Yuuka’s place and avoids replacement logic.

### Relationship with Quang

Quang must be handled with dignity.

If Thiên An moves toward Minh, Quang may still care, but he should not become pathetic or villainized. His quiet withdrawal can hurt more than jealousy.

### Dialogue Style

Precise, restrained, emotionally indirect.

Examples:

```text
“Anh lúc nào cũng nói như thể đã tính trước mọi đường lui.”

“Còn cô thì luôn đứng ở nơi người khác cần, kể cả khi không ai bảo cô phải đứng đó.”
```

```text
“Chúng ta giống nhau sao?”

“Không. Chỉ là cùng biết vài thứ không nên biết quá sớm.”
```

### Writing Rule

Write her as someone drawn to a mirror, not a savior:

```text
She recognizes him.
He recognizes her.
That recognition is dangerous.
Do not make it easy.
```

## 3. Thiên An Single Branch

### Core

Thiên An chooses herself.

This is not a failed romance branch. It is the branch where she refuses to define her future through family duty, romantic rescue, or emotional debt.

She remains responsible, but stops sacrificing herself automatically.

### Personality

* Clearer and calmer.
* Still disciplined, but less trapped by duty.
* More willing to say no.
* More capable of separating love from obligation.
* Learns to rest without guilt.
* Does not become cold; she becomes self-owned.

### Relationship with Family

She still loves her family, but she no longer lets family pressure erase her personal life.

This branch should show her setting boundaries:

* with parents;
* with business expectations;
* with emotional blackmail;
* with her own habit of over-responsibility.

### Relationship with Quang / Minh

Either man may remain important, but Thiên An does not choose romance.

She may respect Quang, understand Minh, or care for both in different ways, but she does not use either as an escape route.

### Dialogue Style

Firm, quiet, not dramatic.

Examples:

```text
“Con sẽ giúp gia đình. Nhưng con không bán cả đời mình cho một quyết định hoảng loạn.”

“Tôi biết anh muốn tốt cho tôi. Nhưng lần này, tôi cần tự quyết.”
```

### Writing Rule

Write her as someone reclaiming authority over her own life:

```text
She still carries responsibility.
But she stops confusing responsibility with surrender.
```

## 4. Thiên An Arranged Marriage Branch

### Core

This is the sacrifice branch.

Thiên An accepts an arranged marriage to protect the family during crisis. She does not do it because she is weak. She does it because pressure, timing, guilt, and duty corner her into choosing the least destructive option.

This branch should feel tragic, not melodramatic.

### Personality

* Most emotionally locked version of Thiên An.
* Calm on the surface, exhausted underneath.
* Makes decisions quickly because hesitation would break her.
* Becomes colder in public.
* Privately shows pain through small mistakes, not speeches.
* Protects others from seeing the cost.

### Relationship with Family

Family pressure overwhelms self-assertion.

She may love her family, but the love becomes a cage. Her choice may save the business or reduce damage, but it costs her personal freedom.

### Relationship with Quang

This is the most painful branch for Quang.

He may try to help, but either:

* he is too late;
* she does not trust him enough;
* his help is not enough;
* or she refuses to let him destroy his own life for her.

Do not write Quang as useless. Write him as someone who cared, but was not allowed close enough in time.

### Relationship with Tâm

Tâm should feel the cost clearly.

This branch can push Tâm to mature, because she sees what happens when silence, pride, and family pressure are left unchecked for too long.

### Dialogue Style

Very controlled. Short lines. Minimal explanation.

Examples:

```text
“Chị đã quyết rồi.”

“Chị có muốn thế không?”

Thiên An looked at the ring box for a moment.

“Muốn không phải câu hỏi còn dùng được nữa.”
```

```text
“Đừng nhìn chị như vậy.”

“Như thế nào?”

“Như thể chị vừa biến mất.”
```

### Writing Rule

Write her as someone who sacrifices without asking to be admired:

```text
She does not collapse.
She does not perform tragedy.
She signs the paper.
Then folds the pen cap slightly wrong.
```

# Quick Comparison

| Branch            | Emotional Core             | Main Dynamic      | Growth Type           | Risk                           |
| ----------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Thiên An × Quang  | trust and receiving help   | patient support   | healthy vulnerability | making Quang a savior          |
| Thiên An × Minh   | recognition through burden | dangerous mirror  | emotional honesty     | replacing Yuuka                |
| Single            | self-ownership             | no forced romance | boundaries            | making it feel like loneliness |
| Arranged Marriage | sacrifice under pressure   | duty over self    | tragic responsibility | making it melodramatic         |

# Agent Rule

Before writing Thiên An, check:

```text
direction_thienan_thienan_quang_commit
direction_thienan_thienan_minh_commit
ach_end_thienan_single
ach_end_thienan_arranged
```

Also check these scores:

```text
thienan_quang_trust
thienan_minh_residue
family_pressure_thienan
thienan_self_assertion
quang_persistence
```

Do not mix branches.

Most common mistake: writing Thiên An as only “the strong sister.”
Avoid this. Her story is about the cost of being useful for too long.
