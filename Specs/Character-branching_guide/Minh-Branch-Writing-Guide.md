# Minh Branch Writing Guide

Minh has three major development branches based on his relationship state with Yuuka:

```text
old_close_friends
ex_lovers_long_separated
still_lovers
```

Each branch changes his emotional texture, dialogue, career direction, and the development of the “Alex” identity.

---

## 1. Old Friends Branch

### Core

Minh and Yuuka were once very close. There was emotional tension, possibly romantic potential, but it never crossed the line into a confirmed relationship.

Minh still has feelings for Yuuka, but they are not strong enough, or not safe enough, for him to leave the comfort zone of friendship.

### Personality

* Reserved and careful.
* Emotionally guarded, but not cold.
* Kind through actions, not declarations.
* Keeps a polite emotional distance.
* Avoids naming feelings directly.
* More stable than the ex-lovers version, but less intimate than the still-lovers version.

### Relationship with Yuuka

He cares about her deeply, but treats that care as friendship.
He remembers small things about her, helps quietly, and notices when she is not okay, but he does not claim any romantic right.

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
almost confessed
almost asked
almost stayed
but stopped before crossing the line
```

### Dialogue Style

Short, restrained, lightly evasive.

Examples:

```text
“Anh vẫn nhớ à?”
“Không khó nhớ.”

“Anh chưa từng nghĩ khác về em sao?”
“Có những chuyện nghĩ rồi cũng không nên nói.”
```

### Career Direction

Minh focuses more directly on his strategic goals.
He does not branch strongly into the artistic or creative side influenced by Yuuka.

He becomes capable, disciplined, and career-focused, but less emotionally transformed by love.

### Alex Identity

“Alex” never fully forms in this branch.

It may exist as a convenient foreign name, but not as a real persona or second identity. Minh has no strong need to split himself into “Minh” and “Alex”.

### Writing Rule

Write him as someone standing one step away from Yuuka:

```text
He remembers.
He cares.
He does not claim.
He does not step forward.
```

## 2. Ex-Lovers Branch

### Core

This is the canon branch.

Minh and Yuuka truly loved each other, then separated. The separation wounded him deeply, but he did not collapse publicly. Instead, he became more controlled, sharper, quieter, and harder to read.

### Personality

* Most emotionally compressed version of Minh.
* Colder on the surface.
* Speaks less, but each line carries weight.
* Still gentle underneath, but heavily guarded.
* Does not punish Yuuka, but does not easily let her back in.
* Remembers everything, while pretending it is not special.

### Relationship with Yuuka

Yuuka is not just someone he loved. She is part of his internal structure.

When they meet again, he should not soften immediately. He must protect himself and test whether she has truly returned, or is only acting from guilt, nostalgia, or fear.

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
still in love
still hurt
still careful
not ready to pretend nothing happened
```

### Dialogue Style

Very restrained. Calm surface, emotional cut underneath.

Examples:

```text
“Anh vẫn nhớ em thích món đó à?”
“Anh chưa đến mức quên hết mọi thứ.”

“Anh giận em sao?”
“Giận là chuyện của lúc còn chờ một lời giải thích.”

“Bây giờ em là khách.”
```

### Career Direction

This version develops the strongest strategic depth.

After losing Yuuka, Minh pours himself into work, systems, influence, and long-term power. He becomes highly capable and difficult to replace, but his personal life becomes narrower.

### Alex Identity

“Alex” forms most clearly in this branch.

Alex is not a fake mask. It is a survival structure:

* more international;
* more socially functional;
* colder and more flexible;
* useful for work, influence, and distance;
* a way for Minh to continue living without being only the man Yuuka left behind.

### Writing Rule

Write him as someone who still loves, but forces every gesture through control:

```text
He remembers too much.
He says too little.
He cares, then hides it.
He will not step forward unless Yuuka truly stays.
```

## 3. Still Lovers Branch

### Core

Yuuka never left. Minh never experienced the four-year separation.

He still has pressure, ambition, and sharp edges, but Yuuka softens him. This is the most human, warm, and emotionally open version of Minh.

### Personality

* Softer and more relaxed.
* Smiles more.
* Teases more.
* Speaks more naturally.
* Allows small vulnerability around Yuuka.
* Still intelligent and controlled, but less defensive at home.
* More visibly affectionate, but not overly sweet.

### Relationship with Yuuka

They are already a couple. The question is not whether they love each other, but how they continue growing without trapping each other.

Their dynamic should feel like:

```text
lived-in intimacy
small arguments
daily care
warm teasing
mutual grounding
```

Minh has the right to care for Yuuka, and he knows it. His affection can be more direct than in the other branches.

### Dialogue Style

Short, warm, dry, lightly teasing.

Examples:

```text
“Anh lại sửa file của em?”
“Anh cứu file của em. Khác nhau khá xa.”

“Anh có thể nói nhớ em một lần tử tế không?”
“Anh vừa nấu món em thích.”
“Đó không phải câu trả lời.”
“Là câu trả lời có ăn được.”
```

### Career Direction

Minh still becomes successful, but less extremely isolated.

Yuuka’s presence keeps him connected to ordinary life and may pull him slightly toward creative or artistic-adjacent work:

* film support;
* production strategy;
* media analysis;
* technical/strategic consulting for creative projects.

He does not become an artist, but his world becomes less purely strategic.

### Alex Identity

“Alex” still appears, but does not dominate.

Alex is a professional identity, not a survival shell. Minh can switch into Alex for work, but he still has a warm personal life to return to.

### Writing Rule

Write him as someone still sharp, but no longer cold:

```text
He is still Minh.
Still controlled.
Still difficult.
But Yuuka gives him a place where he can loosen his grip.
```

# Agent Rule

Before writing Minh, always check:

```text
story_state["minh_yuuka_relation"]
```

Then apply the correct branch voice.

Do not mix branches.

Most common mistake: writing every Minh as canon Ex-Lovers Minh.
Avoid this. His emotional temperature changes heavily by branch.
