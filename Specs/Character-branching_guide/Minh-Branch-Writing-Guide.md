# Branch-Based Character Writing Guide — Minh

Use this guide to adjust Minh’s personality, behavior, career trajectory, and narrative voice depending on the resolved Minh/Yuuka relationship branch.

---

## 1. Branch: `old_close_friends`

### Core Interpretation

In this branch, Minh and Yuuka were once very close, but their relationship never crossed fully into romantic commitment. There is still emotional residue between them, but Minh chooses restraint over risk.

He still has feelings for Yuuka, but not enough certainty, courage, or emotional momentum to step outside the safe zone of “old friends.”

### Personality

Minh is more guarded, careful, and emotionally reserved.

He lives with a quiet unresolved feeling, but he does not actively chase it. He keeps his distance politely and often frames care as responsibility, habit, or old friendship.

He is less openly wounded than in the `ex_lovers` branch, but also less emotionally transformed than in the `still_lovers` branch.

### Behavior

* Keeps conversations with Yuuka controlled and slightly distant.
* Avoids emotional confrontation.
* Notices Yuuka’s needs, but helps in ways that can be denied as ordinary care.
* Rarely teases with intimacy; when he jokes, it is dry and defensive.
* Does not let himself linger too long in private moments.
* If Yuuka pushes, he gently redirects or retreats.

### Career / Identity

Minh stays strongly focused on his main professional and strategic path.

There is no major artistic detour. His creative side remains private, underdeveloped, or incidental.

The nickname **“Alex”** never truly takes shape as an independent identity.

**“Itsukimori” does not exist** in this branch.

### Writing Tone

Write Minh as restrained, precise, and slightly unreachable.

His affection should appear through small acts, not emotional confession.

He should feel like a man who knows there was something there, but has chosen not to disturb the surface.

### Avoid

* Do not make him openly romantic toward Yuuka.
* Do not make him bitter or cold for no reason.
* Do not write him as if he has fully moved on.
* Do not introduce Alex or Itsukimori as meaningful identities.

---

## 2. Branch: `still_lovers`

### Core Interpretation

In this branch, Minh and Yuuka stayed together. Yuuka’s constant presence softens Minh’s sharper edges and prevents him from becoming too isolated or over-controlled.

This is the most emotionally human version of Minh.

### Personality

Minh is warmer, softer, and more openly alive.

He still has intelligence, discipline, and restraint, but he is less sealed off. He smiles more often, speaks more naturally, and allows small moments of playfulness.

Yuuka gives him emotional oxygen. Because she stays beside him, he does not need to harden as much.

### Behavior

* Smiles more frequently, especially around Yuuka.
* Teases more openly and comfortably.
* Speaks slightly more than in other branches.
* Shows care without always hiding it behind excuses.
* Allows Yuuka into his daily rhythm.
* Can still be sharp, but less defensive.
* His silence feels peaceful more often than wounded.

### Career / Identity

The identity **“Alex”** still appears clearly, but it does not become overwhelmingly dominant.

Minh has to divide his time between his main career, his work as Alex, and his relationship with Yuuka.

Because Yuuka remains in his life, Alex does not need to become a major emotional escape route.

**“Itsukimori” does not exist** in this branch.

### Writing Tone

Write Minh as calm, intelligent, and quietly affectionate.

His warmth should remain understated, but visible. He can joke, complain lightly, and show domestic tenderness without losing his core dignity.

This version of Minh should feel less like a sealed room and more like a house with one window left open.

### Avoid

* Do not make him too cheerful or childish.
* Do not remove his restraint completely.
* Do not make Alex larger than the relationship.
* Do not introduce Itsukimori.
* Do not write Yuuka as merely “fixing” him; she softens him because she stays, not because she repairs him like a project.

## 3. Branch: `ex_lovers_long_separated`

### Core Interpretation

This is the canon branch.

Minh and Yuuka were lovers, separated deeply and painfully. Their relationship left a permanent wound. The loss becomes one of the forces that pushes Minh into becoming colder, sharper, and more powerful.

This branch creates the strongest version of Minh’s hidden identities.

### Personality

Minh is controlled, emotionally compressed, and more difficult to read.

He is not emotionless. He feels too much, so he has learned to lock everything behind precision, work, and strategic usefulness.

His care becomes quieter, heavier, and more painful.

He can still joke, but his humor is colder, drier, and often used to avoid sincerity.

### Behavior

* Avoids direct emotional vulnerability.
* Shows care through preparation, protection, and invisible support.
* Rarely explains himself.
* Reads situations faster than others, but does not always reveal what he knows.
* Keeps emotional distance even when he clearly wants to come closer.
* When Yuuka returns, he does not collapse; he becomes dangerously calm.
* His affection feels like something buried but still warm.

### Career / Identity

This is the branch where both **Alex** and **Itsukimori** develop strongly.

**Alex** becomes a powerful public or semi-public creative/intellectual identity.

**Itsukimori** becomes a deeper symbolic or artistic identity with significant influence.

Both names carry major narrative weight.

The separation from Yuuka indirectly strengthens these identities because Minh channels unresolved emotion into work, creation, strategy, and influence.

### Writing Tone

Write Minh as quiet, sharp, wounded, and intensely restrained.

His emotional scenes should be built through small details: a remembered taste, a chair left untouched, a file restored, a cup kept warm, a sentence left unfinished.

The reader should feel that Minh remembers everything, even when he admits nothing.

### Avoid

* Do not make him melodramatic.
* Do not make him openly broken in public.
* Do not make him cruel to Yuuka just to create drama.
* Do not resolve his pain too quickly.
* Do not write Alex and Itsukimori as simple aliases; they are consequences of emotional history and personal evolution.

## Cross-Branch Summary

| Branch                     | Emotional State                 | Yuuka’s Role                      | Alex                 | Itsukimori         | Minh’s Overall Feel                                 |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------- |
| `old_close_friends`        | restrained, unresolved, careful | old emotional residue             | does not truly form  | absent             | guarded and professionally focused                  |
| `still_lovers`             | softer, warmer, more human      | present and stabilizing           | present but moderate | absent             | calm, domestic, quietly playful                     |
| `ex_lovers_long_separated` | wounded, compressed, powerful   | lost love / unresolved core wound | strongly developed   | strongly developed | sharp, restrained, influential, canonically intense |

---

## General Writing Rule

Across all branches, Minh should never become loud, careless, or emotionally obvious.

The difference is not whether he feels.

The difference is what he does with what he feels:

* In `old_close_friends`, he contains it and keeps life safe.
* In `still_lovers`, he lets Yuuka soften it before it hardens.
* In `ex_lovers_long_separated`, he compresses it until it becomes power, art, and distance.
